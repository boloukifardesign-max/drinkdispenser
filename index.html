About

An automated, precision beverage dispensing and mixing platform engineered to deliver flavor consistency, rapid service, and streamlined inventory management. Featuring a multi-channel fluid handling system, precise tank calibration, and flexible recipe formulation built on core base liquids, the platform guarantees exact pour ratios with every cycle. Designed with a modular fluidic architecture and automated maintenance routines (Service Mode), it ensures optimal hygiene standards and long-term hardware reliability under continuous use.
